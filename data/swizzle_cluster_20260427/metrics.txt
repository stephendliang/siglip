Legend:
  bij    = bijective tile coverage (X = duplicate or missing tiles)
  tmRun  = mean intra-cluster run length of equal tm (consecutive same-A; matters only for SMEM-prefetch)
  tnRun  = mean intra-cluster run length of equal tn (consecutive same-B; B is L2-resident, ≥1 fine)
  uTm    = mean unique tm count per 74-wavefront (lower = denser within-wavefront A multicast)
  uTn    = mean unique tn count per wavefront (3.0 = ALL tn columns active per tick — best B parallelism)
  tmMult = mean clusters-per-shared-tm at one tick (high = each A strip multicasts to many clusters)
  frP50  = median fresh (never-seen) tm strips per wavefront — DRAM influx rate
  frMax  = peak fresh tm strips per wavefront — peak DRAM burst, large = stalls
  L2w8   = fraction of (cluster, tm) at tick T whose tm was issued in [T-8, T-1] — short-window L2 warmth
  tmExt  = mean (max_tm - min_tm + 1) per wavefront — small = tight DRAM page locality
  tmDen  = mean uniq_tm / tm_extent per wavefront — how 'dense' the wavefront's M-coverage is in M-space
  tnQu   = mean (peak same-tn mass)/74 per wavefront — high = many clusters queueing on same B-strip
